Built for how students actually read

SmartShelf started as a final-year project and grew into a full digital library platform — because campus libraries deserved better software.

Our Mission

Make discovering, borrowing and returning books as easy as ordering food — no queues, no confusing catalog cards, just a search bar that works.

What We Offer

A searchable digital catalog, real-time availability, personal borrowing history, and a curated feed of what's actually trending with students.

Who It's For

Students who want their textbooks and their BookTok reads in the same place. Librarians who want less paperwork and more visibility.

Always Free for Students

SmartShelf is a campus utility, not a product. Borrowing, browsing and account creation will always be free for students and staff.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long can I keep a book?

Every borrow comes with a 14-day loan period from the day you check it out, visible on your dashboard.

Can I reserve a book that's out?

Not yet — but it's on our roadmap! For now, check back on the book's details page for availability.

Who adds new books?

Library staff manage the catalog through the admin panel, including new arrivals and trending picks.

Is my data safe?

This demo build stores your account and borrowing activity locally in your browser — nothing leaves your device.
